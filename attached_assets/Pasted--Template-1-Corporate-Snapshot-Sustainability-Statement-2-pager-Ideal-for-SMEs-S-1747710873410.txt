## ✅ Template 1: **Corporate Snapshot Sustainability Statement (2-pager)**

**Ideal for:** SMEs, Startups, and B2B SaaS Companies
**Design:** One-page visual company overview + one-page ESG performance table

### 🔹 Page 1 – Executive Summary

* **Company Logo** (top left)
* **Tagline** (top right, e.g. “Empowering Sustainable Growth”)
* **About Us** (50–75 words)
* **Sustainability Vision & Strategy** (short paragraph)
* **UN SDG Alignment** (icons or bullet list)
* **Image Placeholder:** Photo of team/product/impact work

### 🔹 Page 2 – ESG Snapshot

| Metric Category | Indicator                         | 2023   | 2022  |
| --------------- | --------------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Climate         | Total GHG Emissions (tCO2e)       | 1,245  | 1,400 |
|                 | % Renewable Energy Used           | 65%    | 42%   |
| Social          | Female Leadership (%)             | 52%    | 48%   |
|                 | Community Investment (\$)         | \$120K | \$85K |
| Governance      | ESG Board Oversight (Yes/No)      | Yes    | Yes   |
|                 | Ethics Hotline Cases Resolved (%) | 98%    | 96%   |

---

### ✏️ Sample Report (Template 1 – Summary)

**Company Name:** Greentech AI
**About Us:** Greentech AI is a clean-tech software firm enabling smart energy optimization for factories.
**Sustainability Vision:** We aim to reduce 1 million tonnes of CO₂e by 2030 through AI-powered interventions.
**SDGs Aligned:** SDG 7 (Affordable Clean Energy), SDG 9 (Innovation), SDG 13 (Climate Action)
**Key Metrics:**

* GHG emissions cut by 11% YoY
* 65% of energy from renewables
* \$120K invested in local STEM programs

## ✅ Template 2: **Visual Impact Report (2-page, Infographic Style)**

**Ideal for:** External-facing sustainability statements, impact startups
**Design:** Split into quadrants per theme

### 🔹 Page 1 – Quadrant Summary (Icons + Stats)

* **Climate**

  * 🌱 Net Emissions: 1,400 tCO₂e
  * ♻️ Renewable Energy: 68%
* **People**

  * 👩‍💼 Women in Workforce: 54%
  * 🧑🏽‍💻 Training Hours: 1,200 hrs
* **Governance**

  * ✅ ESG Policy Adopted: 2022
  * 💼 ESG-linked Bonus: Introduced 2023
* **Future Targets**

  * Net-Zero by 2040
  * Zero-waste packaging by 2026

### 🔹 Page 2 – Story Section

* “Our Journey to Sustainability” – narrative with images and quotes
* “Looking Ahead” – milestones timeline
* “Our Commitments” – short text + SDG icons
* **Image placeholders:** 1 landscape banner + 2 smaller photos

---

### ✏️ Sample Report (Template 2 – Impact Visual)

**Our Climate Story:**
Since launching in 2019, SolarX has helped 400+ schools cut 1,200 tCO₂e. 68% of our HQ energy is solar-sourced.

**People & Inclusion:**
54% of staff are women. In 2023, all employees received DEI training and mental wellness benefits.

**Looking Forward:**

* Launch Asia pilot by 2025
* Publish Scope 3 inventory
* Achieve net-zero by 2040

## ✅ Template 3: **ESG Strategy and Targets Brief**

**Ideal for:** ESG consultants, B2B proposals, banks, SMEs
**Design:** Structured sections with clear headers + chart blocks

### 🔹 Page 1

* **Sustainability Strategy** (1–2 paragraphs)
* **Governance Model** – chart of roles/responsibilities
* **Risks & Opportunities Summary Table**

### 🔹 Page 2

* **Climate Targets Timeline** (e.g. bar or line chart)
* **KPIs & Metrics Table**
* **Material Topics Matrix (visual placeholder)**

---

### ✏️ Sample Report (Template 3 – Strategic Brief)

**Strategy:**
EcoWare’s ESG program focuses on circular design, supplier traceability, and emissions reduction in packaging. We conduct annual risk mapping tied to material topics and TCFD guidance.

**Governance:**

* ESG Committee: Monthly reviews
* CFO: Climate scenario planning

**Climate Targets:**

* -30% Scope 1 & 2 by 2027
* Full LCAs for all products by 2026

**Top Material Topics:**

* Climate resilience
* Supplier human rights
* Packaging footprint
