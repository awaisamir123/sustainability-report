Section	Description

Management’s Role	C-suite roles, risk management team, ESG committee, KPIs.

🔹 2. Strategy
Element	Content

Impact on Business	Value chain, geographies, products
Time Horizons	Short (0–2 yrs), Medium (3–5 yrs), Long (5+ yrs)
Resilience Strategy	Use of scenario analysis (e.g., 1.5°C, 2°C)

✏️ Sample TCFD Report (Generic Company)
Company: ClimateAware Corp
Sector: Textiles
Report Year: 2023
Status: Second year of TCFD-aligned reporting

🔸 Executive Summary:
ClimateAware Corp recognizes climate change as a core financial risk. In 2023, we assessed our Scope 3 emissions, implemented a 1.5°C scenario analysis, and adopted board-level climate KPIs.

🔸 Governance:
Board reviews climate risks quarterly.

ESG Committee includes the CFO, COO, and Head of Sustainability.

KPI: % of CapEx aligned to climate mitigation.

🔸 Strategy:
Risks: Increased raw material costs (cotton), regulatory pressure in EU

Opportunities: Green products, renewable energy PPA

Resilience modeled via 1.5°C and 4°C scenarios

📊 Scenario chart comparing GHG emissions under each scenario

🔸 Risk Management:
Risks assessed quarterly using a probability x impact matrix

Physical risks prioritized by region (e.g., flooding in Southeast Asia)

📊 Top 5 climate risks in heatmap format

🔸 Metrics & Targets:
Metric	2023	2022
Scope 1 (tCO₂e)	850	1,000
Scope 2 (tCO₂e)	600	750
Scope 3 (tCO₂e)	5,200	N/A
% Renewable Energy	65%	40%

🎯 Target: Net-zero by 2040 (interim: -50% by 2030)
